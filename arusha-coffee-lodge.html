Arusha Coffee Lodge

Arusha Coffee Lodge is a luxurious and tranquil retreat located at the heart of a working coffee plantation in Arusha, Tanzania. Set against the backdrop of Mount Meru, the lodge offers guests a unique experience, combining the charm of rural Tanzania with modern comforts. Guests can stay in beautifully designed rooms and suites, each featuring rustic décor and stunning views of the lush coffee fields. The lodge provides an immersive experience with coffee-related activities, such as coffee tours and tastings, where visitors can learn about the production process from bean to cup. In addition to its exceptional accommodations, Arusha Coffee Lodge boasts a gourmet restaurant, a spa, and a serene atmosphere, making it an ideal spot for relaxation or as a base for exploring nearby national parks like Serengeti and Ngorongoro Crater.
