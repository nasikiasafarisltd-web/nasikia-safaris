Popular Day Trip Options Include:

  • Arusha National Park Game Drive: Spot diverse wildlife in a scenic setting.
  • Materuni Waterfalls & Coffee Tour: Hike to stunning waterfalls and learn about coffee production.
  • Tarangire National Park: Witness large herds of elephants and picturesque baobab trees.
  • Lake Manyara National Park: Explore its diverse habitats and birdlife.
  • Kilimanjaro Day Hike: Experience a short trek on Africa’s tallest peak.
