Climbing Kilimanjaro, Africa's tallest peak!

Climbing Kilimanjaro is more than just reaching the summit; it’s about experiencing the beauty and diversity of the mountain and its surroundings. Choosing the right Kilimanjaro routes is vital for a successful journey.

Mount Kilimanjaro, situated in the northern part of Tanzania within the Kilimanjaro region, stands as Africa's tallest mountain and the world's highest free-standing peak, reaching a height of 5,895 meters (19,341 feet) with its main summit named Uhuru Peak. Accessible via the town of Moshi, which can be reached either by plane or road from Arusha, Kilimanjaro offers a remarkable climbing experience. While reaching the summit typically takes 4 to 5 days, rapid ascents may pose challenges due to the increased risk of altitude sickness.
