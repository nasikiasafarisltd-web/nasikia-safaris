Full Days Relaxation in Nungwi(Northern Coast of Zanzibar)

Nungwi, nestled on the northern tip of Zanzibar, boasts pristine white sands and crystal-clear turquoise waters, creating an idyllic setting for your honeymoon retreat. Here, you have the choice to luxuriate in your hotel accommodation or immerse yourself in the refreshing ocean waters. Should you seek adventure, activities such as snorkeling, dolphin tours, safari blue excursions, and diving await, either arranged through us or directly through your hotel with expert guidance. Notably, Nungwi’s tidal changes offer a unique tranquility distinct from other areas, ensuring a serene experience throughout your five-night stay.
