Our 10 Best Luxury Safari Camps & Lodges in the Serengeti: Highlights & Locations

The Serengeti is a dream destination for travelers seeking an authentic, awe-inspiring African safari experience. Known for its vast plains, exceptional wildlife, and the iconic Great Migration, the Serengeti offers a magical backdrop for luxury safaris. With world-class camps and lodges, you can experience this breathtaking landscape in unparalleled comfort. Whether you're seeking seclusion, proximity to wildlife, or top-tier service, these 10 luxury safari camps and lodges in the Serengeti are sure to provide a once-in-a-lifetime adventure. In this guide, we’ll explore the best luxury accommodations, what makes them unique, and the most luxurious experiences they offer.

Ready to Start Planning Your Serengeti Safari?

Planning a Serengeti safari in 2025 is an exciting step toward experiencing some of the world’s most incredible wildlife and landscapes. The Serengeti is known for its diverse wildlife, stunning landscapes, and rich cultural heritage. For those looking to make their safari unforgettable, staying at a luxury lodge or camp offers more than just comfort—it’s about elevating the experience.

Whether you want to witness the annual Great Migration of over 1.5 million wildebeests and zebras or watch a pride of lions on the hunt from your private deck, luxury safari camps and lodges in the Serengeti are strategically placed to provide you with the best views and the most intimate encounters with nature. Ready to start your adventure? Here’s a closer look at the 10 best luxury safari camps and lodges in the Serengeti, designed to help you choose the perfect one for your safari dreams!

1. Four Seasons Safari Lodge Serengeti – Luxury in the Heart of the Serengeti

Four Seasons Safari Lodge Serengeti offers the perfect balance of relaxation and adventure. Located in central Serengeti, this lodge is surrounded by wildlife and offers stunning views of the savannah. Guests can witness elephants, giraffes, and other animals right outside their windows, especially near the waterholes that attract a variety of wildlife. In addition to luxurious accommodations, Four Seasons offers a state-of-the-art spa, a fitness center, and a gorgeous infinity pool overlooking the plains. This lodge is perfect for those who want the best of both worlds: luxury and wildlife immersion.

Location: Central Serengeti, near the Seronera River
Highlights: Stunning waterhole views, world-class spa, family-friendly accommodations, infinity pool with wildlife sightings.

2. Singita Sasakwa Lodge – Ultimate Serengeti Luxury and Exclusivity

For the discerning traveler looking for ultimate luxury, Singita Sasakwa Lodge is a dream come true. Perched on a hill with sweeping panoramic views of the Serengeti, this exclusive lodge is one of the most opulent options in the park. With only 9 suites and a private villa, Singita Sasakwa offers privacy and sophistication. The lodge boasts a magnificent wine cellar, an infinity pool, and gourmet cuisine that can be enjoyed while observing wildlife from your suite’s terrace. Additionally, guests can enjoy private game drives and unique experiences like stargazing or a hot air balloon ride over the Serengeti.

Location: Grumeti Reserves, Western Serengeti
Highlights: Panoramic views, gourmet dining, private safari experiences, wine cellar, hot air balloon rides.

3. Beyond Serengeti Under Canvas – A Luxurious Mobile Safari Experience

For those who want to experience the thrill of a mobile safari without sacrificing comfort, &Beyond Serengeti Under Canvas is a unique option. This luxury mobile camp shifts locations to follow the Great Migration, bringing guests closer to the wildlife spectacle. The spacious, tented suites feature en-suite bathrooms, king-sized beds, and private verandas with views of the Serengeti’s vast plains. With personalized service and expertly guided game drives, this camp provides an intimate, immersive experience for those who love adventure but still crave luxury.

Location: Various locations within the Serengeti, moving with the migration.
Highlights: Mobile camp setup, intimate safari experience, front-row seats to the Great Migration, private verandas.

4. Tarangire Treetops – Elevated Luxury in the Heart of Nature

While not technically within the Serengeti, Tarangire Treetops offers a unique luxury experience just outside the park in the Tarangire National Park. Staying in treehouse-style suites elevated above the ground, guests enjoy exceptional views of the surrounding landscapes and wildlife below. The lodge is perfect for those who want to enjoy the beauty of nature from a high vantage point in a secluded, eco-friendly environment. The on-site game drives and walking safaris bring guests up close to wildlife, making it a great choice for those seeking both serenity and adventure.

Location: Tarangire National Park (near Serengeti).
Highlights:Elevated treehouses, eco-friendly design, game drives, walking safaris, birdwatching.

5. The Serengeti Bushtops – A Luxurious Safari Retreat with Exceptional Views

The Serengeti Bushtops is an exclusive, luxurious retreat set in a stunning location that offers exceptional views of the Serengeti’s vast landscapes. The camp’s spacious tents come with private plunge pools, large verandas, and en-suite bathrooms, offering the perfect setting to relax after a thrilling game drive. The camp's gourmet dining, private safari experiences, and stunning sundowner locations create the perfect environment for luxury and adventure. Serengeti Bushtops is especially known for its personalized service, with every guest’s needs attended to by their own private butler.

Location: Western Serengeti, near the Mara River.
Highlights: Private plunge pools, personalized service, views of the Great Migration, luxury dining, spa services.

6. Sanctuary Kusini Camp – A Remote Escape with Exceptional Migration Viewing

Located in the remote southern Serengeti, Sanctuary Kusini Camp is known for offering a more intimate and secluded safari experience. As one of the few camps located in this quieter area, guests have the unique opportunity to experience the Serengeti’s wildlife without large crowds. The camp provides outstanding views of the Great Migration, as well as exceptional opportunities for wildlife photography. With only 12 tents, it offers an intimate, exclusive experience with personalized service and private game drives.

Location: Southern Serengeti.
Highlights: Intimate atmosphere, excellent migration views, private game drives, peaceful setting.

7. Serengeti Migration Camp – A Front-Row Seat to the Great Migration

As its name suggests, Serengeti Migration Camp offers prime positioning for experiencing the Great Migration. The camp is located near the heart of the migration path, offering one of the best opportunities to witness the incredible wildebeest and zebra herds that traverse the Serengeti each year. The luxurious tents feature expansive views of the surrounding wilderness and provide a comfortable, high-end base for safari enthusiasts who want to be in the middle of the action while enjoying plush amenities.

Location: Central Serengeti, near the migration route.
Highlights: Prime Great Migration viewing, luxury tented accommodations, walking safaris, private game drives.

8. Olakira Camp – A Mobile Camp That Moves with the Migration

Olakira Camp offers the ultimate flexibility by moving with the seasonal migration, allowing guests to experience the Serengeti’s wildlife wherever the action is. The camp’s spacious tents are equipped with en-suite bathrooms, comfortable furnishings, and stunning views of the Serengeti plains. The mobile nature of Olakira Camp means that guests are guaranteed to be positioned in prime areas for wildlife viewing, making it a great choice for those who want an authentic and dynamic safari experience.

Location: Northern & Southern Serengeti (seasonal).
Highlights: Seasonal migration positioning, intimate and remote, luxury mobile camp, wildlife immersion.

9. Nomad Serengeti Camp – The Best of Both Worlds: Luxury and Adventure

Nomad Serengeti Camp is a mobile camp that offers guests the best of both worlds: luxury accommodations in the heart of the Serengeti’s wilderness. The camp features spacious, stylish tents with en-suite bathrooms and private verandas for ultimate comfort. Guests are treated to incredible game drives, walking safaris, and the opportunity to experience the Serengeti in a less-crowded, more intimate environment.

Location: Various locations within the Serengeti (seasonal).
Highlights: Seasonally changing locations, luxury tented accommodations, personalized safari experiences, expert guides.

10. Dunia Camp – Exclusive and Secluded in the Northern Serengeti

Dunia Camp is one of the most exclusive camps in the northern Serengeti, offering a peaceful and intimate experience away from the crowds. With just 8 luxury tents, Dunia ensures a quiet, serene experience where you can enjoy the surrounding wilderness in complete privacy. The camp is positioned for prime Great Migration viewing, and guests can enjoy game drives, walking safaris, and sun-downer drinks while watching wildlife.

Location: Northern Serengeti, Kogatende region.
Highlights: Exclusive atmosphere, Great Migration views, private and quiet experience, intimate luxury.
