The 7 Days, 6 Nights Kilimanjaro Trekking Machame Route is one of the most popular and scenic routes to the summit of Mount Kilimanjaro. Known as the "Whiskey Route" due to its challenging terrain, it offers stunning views of the mountain’s landscapes, from lush rainforests to the stark, rocky terrain of the alpine desert. With a gradual ascent and time for acclimatization, this route enhances the chances of a successful summit at Uhuru Peak, standing at 5,895 meters. Ideal for adventurous trekkers seeking a rewarding challenge.

Detail Itinerary

1

Arrival at the Airport

After arrival at the airport (Kilimanjaro / Arusha), your private driver guide will pick you up at the airport and transfer you to your accommodation for dinner and overnight awaiting for your departure on the following day. Your safari guide will meet you at your accommodation for the briefing and equipment checkup.

Arusha

Arusha Planet Lodge

Bed & Break

2

Arusha to Machame Gate - Machame Camp

In the early morning, we depart for Machame Gate, a journey of approximately 90 minutes. Upon arrival, lunch is served at the gate before commencing our ascent. The trail leads us directly into the lush rainforest, where the likelihood of rain can transform the path into a muddy and slippery experience. Often, our porters and cooks reach the campsite ahead of us to prepare meals and set up tents. Be prepared for colder temperatures at Machame Camp, especially during the night.

Habitat: Montane Forest

Duration: 5 hours

Distance: About 7 km

3

Machame Camp to Shira Cave

Following breakfast, we trek towards Shira Cave (3750m), where a hot lunch awaits. The trail ascends through the forest for about an hour, then continues through the moorland zone for approximately two hours. Along the way, enjoy views of the Western Breach and Kibo. Upon reaching Shira Cave in the evening, settle in for the night.

Habitat: Moorland

Hiking time: 5 hours

Distance: About 7 km

4

Shira - Lava Tower - Barranco Camp

Today’s route heads east into a rocky semi-desert landscape surrounding Lava Tower, reaching an altitude of 4600m after about five hours of walking. Lunch is served before ascending the rocky scree path to Lava Tower. This is typically the toughest day, with some climbers experiencing symptoms of altitude sickness. Descend to Barranco Camp, situated below the Breach and Great Barranco Wall, for dinner and overnight stay.

Habitat: Semi-Desert

Hiking time: 7 hours

Distance: About 11 km

5

Barranco Camp to Karanga Camp to Barafu Camp

After breakfast, ascend steep ridges, passing the challenging Barranco Wall, towards Karanga Valley Camp. Following lunch, continue towards Barafu Camp, marking the completion of the South Circuit. Enjoy panoramic views of Mawenzi and Kibo peaks from this vantage point. Rest, dine, and prepare for the summit day ahead.

Habitat: Alpine desert

Hiking time: 8 hours

Distance: About 10 km

6

Summit Attempt, Barafu Camp - Uhuru Peak - Mweka

Wake up around 2300hrs for the summit climb. Ascend towards Stella Point on the crater rim, then continue to Uhuru Peak. Enjoy the sunrise and breathtaking views before descending to Barafu Camp. After a brief rest, descend to Mweka Hut through rock and scree paths into the moorland and forest. Dinner and washing water will be prepared at Mweka Camp.

Habitat: Stone Scree and Ice-Capped Summit

Hiking time: 7 hours

Distance: About 14 km

7

Mweka Camp - Mweka Gate

After breakfast, embark on a scenic three-hour hike back to Mweka Gate. Sign your name in the register and receive your summit certificate. Celebrate with your mountain crew before being transferred to a hotel in Moshi or Arusha for a hot shower and dinner. Alternatively, transfer to Kilima2njaro International Airport for your return flight home in the even.

Habitat: Forest

Hiking time: 3 hours

Distance: About 2 km
