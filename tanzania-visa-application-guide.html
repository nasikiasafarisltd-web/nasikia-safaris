Tanzania Visa Application Guide: How to Apply for a Visa to Tanzania in 2025

If you're planning to visit Tanzania in 2025, one of the first steps is to secure your Tanzania visa. Whether you're traveling for tourism, business, or other purposes, applying for a Tanzania visa is a crucial part of your travel preparation. In this detailed guide, we’ll cover everything you need to know about the Tanzania visa application process, including visa types, requirements, fees, and tips for a successful application. Whether you're applying for an eVisa or a visa on arrival, this comprehensive guide will help you navigate the process with ease.

What is the Tanzania Visa Application Process?

The Tanzania visa application process is designed to be simple and straightforward, especially with the introduction of the eVisa system. Depending on your nationality, the type of visa, and the purpose of your visit, the application process can vary. Understanding these factors and having all the required documents in order will make your visa application process smooth and hassle-free.

With so much to offer, Tanzania is a must-visit for any traveler seeking adventure, wildlife, and natural beauty.

Types of Tanzania Visas You Can Apply For

Before you begin your Tanzania visa application, it’s essential to understand the different types of visas available. Tanzania offers several visa categories, each with specific requirements. Here are the most common types:

  • 1. Tourist Visa: This is the most common type of visa, intended for travelers visiting Tanzania for leisure, sightseeing, or holidays. It is available as a single-entry or multiple-entry visa, depending on your travel plans.
  • 2. Business Visa: If you are visiting Tanzania for work purposes, business meetings, conferences, or trade shows, you’ll need to apply for a business visa.
  • 3. Transit Visa: For travelers passing through Tanzania on their way to another destination, the transit visa is required. It’s usually valid for 7 days.
  • 4. Student Visa: If you plan to study in Tanzania, you will need a student visa. This requires proof of admission from a Tanzanian educational institution.
  • 5. East African Tourist Visa: : If you are planning to visit multiple East African countries (Tanzania, Kenya, and Uganda), you can apply for an East African Tourist Visa. This allows you to travel between the three countries with a single visa.

How to Apply for a Tanzania Visa: Step-by-Step Process

1. Determine Which Type of Visa You Need

Before applying for your visa, ensure you select the correct visa type based on the purpose of your visit. Each visa type has specific requirements and documentation.

2. Apply for the Tanzania eVisa

The easiest and most efficient way to apply for a Tanzania visa is through the eVisa system. The Tanzania government offers an online visa application that allows travelers to apply for a visa before they arrive in the country.

To apply for a Tanzania eVisa, follow these steps:

  • Visit the Official Tanzania eVisa Portal: Go to the official eVisa website and create an account.
  • Fill Out the Application Form: Provide the necessary personal and travel information, including your passport details, travel dates, and reason for visiting.
  • Upload Required Documents: You will need to upload a few important documents, such as a copy of your passport (with at least six months validity), a recent passport-sized photo, and proof of accommodation.
  • Pay the Visa Fee: Visa fees vary depending on the type of visa and your nationality. Payment can be made online via credit or debit card.
  • Submit Your Application: Once all the required information and documents are provided, submit your application.
3. Visa Processing Time

The processing time for a Tanzania eVisa typically takes between 7 to 10 business days. It’s recommended to apply for your visa at least two to three weeks in advance of your travel date to avoid any last-minute delays.

4. Receive Your eVisa Approval

Once your application is approved, you will receive your eVisa approval letter via email. Be sure to print this approval and carry it with you when traveling to Tanzania. It must be presented upon arrival at the immigration counter.

5. Visa on Arrival Option

If you are not eligible for the eVisa or prefer not to apply online, Tanzania also offers a visa on arrival option at some international airports and land borders. However, this process may be time-consuming, and there’s no guarantee that you’ll be granted a visa, so it’s safer to apply in advance.

Tanzania Visa Requirements: What Documents Do You Need?

To apply for a Tanzania visa, whether online or in person, you will need the following documents:

  • Passport: Your passport must be valid for at least six months beyond your planned departure date from Tanzania.
  • Passport-Sized Photographs: Two recent passport-sized photos that meet the specifications outlined on the eVisa portal.
  • Travel Itinerary: A copy of your flight itinerary showing your arrival and departure dates in Tanzania.
  • Accommodation Proof: A hotel booking or a letter of invitation from a host in Tanzania. .
  • Visa Fee Payment: Proof of payment of the visa fee, which can be done through the eVisa portal.
  • Travel Insurance: Though not always mandatory, some applicants may need to show proof of travel insurance, especially for medical emergencies
  • Additional Documents: Depending on the visa type, you may need additional documents, such as an invitation letter for business travelers or a letter of acceptance for students.
Tanzania Visa Fees: What Does It Cost?

Visa fees for Tanzania vary based on the type of visa you are applying for and your nationality. Below are approximate fees for common visa types:

  • Tourist Visa (Single Entry): Around $50 USD.
  • Business Visa: Typically $250 USD.
  • Transit Visa: Usually $30 USD.
  • Multiple Entry Visa: Around $100 USD for one year.
  • East African Tourist Visa: Approximately $100 USD.

It’s important to verify the exact fee on the official eVisa portal or through the Tanzanian embassy, as fees can change.

Tips for a Successful Tanzania Visa Application
  • Check Passport Validity: Ensure your passport is valid for at least six months beyond your intended departure from Tanzania. Renew it if necessary before applying for your visa.
  • Apply Early: To avoid last-minute complications, apply for your Tanzania visa at least 2-3 weeks before your travel date.
  • Double-Check Document Requirements: Incomplete or incorrect documents can delay your visa processing. Ensure you have all the required paperwork, and double-check the specifications.
  • Stay Informed About Policy Changes: Visa policies can change, so make sure to check the official Tanzania visa website regularly for the latest information.

The Tanzania visa application process is straightforward if you follow the necessary steps and ensure that all your documents are in order. By applying for an eVisa or visa on arrival in advance, you can avoid delays and have more time to enjoy your adventure in Tanzania. Whether you’re exploring the Serengeti, climbing Mount Kilimanjaro, or relaxing on the beaches of Zanzibar, securing your visa will be the first step towards an unforgettable journey. For up-to-date information and to apply for your Tanzania visa, visit the official Tanzania eVisa website today!
